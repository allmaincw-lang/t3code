# Bundled models

- `robot.glb` — RobotExpressive by Tomás Laulhé (Quaternius), modifications by Don McCurdy. CC0 1.0.
  From the three.js examples.
- `crystal.glb`, `knot.glb` — original shapes generated for this project from plain three.js geometry. CC0 1.0.

Only CC0 models belong here. Larger CC0 glTF samples (Khronos glTF-Sample-Assets: ToyCar, BoomBox, Lantern,
AntiqueCamera) are 5 to 17 MB each, so they are not bundled.
